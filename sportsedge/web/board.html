The brain

Funky Buttlovin prices every spread, over/under, moneyline and player prop. Probabilities come from team ratings fitted to every final score (recent games weigh more; back-to-backs, home field and key numbers like 3 and 7 learned from data; MLB starting pitchers), player box scores for props (opponent matchups, teammates out), and the no-vig consensus of the sportsbooks. The blend between our model and the market is re-learned from graded picks.

Closing-line value (CLV) is the long-run tell: if our prices beat the closing line, the edge is real. No system wins every bet. Bet only what you can afford to lose, and only where it is legal for you.
